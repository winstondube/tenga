# Design

## Theme

Two surfaces, one system.

**The landing hero is drenched** in a near-black viridian and stays dark in both light and dark themes. It is the one place the brand raises its voice. Everything behind it, the request flow, tracking and operations, sits on a light off-white by default and follows the viewer's theme preference.

The scene that decided it: a woman in Harare, on her phone at nine at night, on data she is counting, about to pay a stranger in advance for a bottle of perfume she cannot hold. She needs it to look like a real business and read at arm's length in a lit room. A committed dark hero establishes the first; a light, high-contrast application does the second.

## Color

Strategy: **Committed.** One saturated colour carries the hero; the application is restrained tinted neutrals plus that same accent.

| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--drench` | `#0B2620` | `#081D19` | Hero ground. Dark in both themes by design |
| `--gold` | `#E0AE5C` | `#E0AE5C` | Hero accent. Fixed, because the ground never lightens |
| `--accent` | `#0B5D4E` | `#4FBFA4` | The UK leg. Approved, confirmed, cleared |
| `--ochre` | `#B57A1F` | `#D9A64E` | The Zimbabwe leg. Cargo, customs, arrival |
| `--bg` | `#F0F3F0` | `#0C1211` | Page ground, biased green rather than warm |
| `--ink` | `#12181A` | `#EDEFEC` | Body text |

Semantics (`--danger`, `--warn`, `--info`) are separate from the accent and never borrow it.

**The two-leg split is structural, not decorative.** Green is everything up to the UK warehouse; ochre is everything after the handover to cargo. The tracking timeline literally changes colour at that point, because the handover is the moment customers worry about. Colour is never the only carrier: each leg is also labelled and positioned.

The neutral is deliberately biased toward the brand's green rather than toward warmth. A warm near-white was the first instinct and it is the saturated default; it was rejected.

## Typography

No webfonts. The primary user is on metered mobile data, so the payload is not worth it. System stacks used with conviction instead.

| Role | Stack | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Georgia, Iowan Old Style, serif | The hero headline and nothing else |
| Body | system sans (`-apple-system`, Segoe UI) | All prose, labels, controls |
| Data | system mono (SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas) | Every price, weight, reference and order number |

**The split carries the personality.** Prose is warm and set in serif or humanist sans; anything that is a figure is monospaced with `tabular-nums` so columns of money line up. Warm surface, precise bones, made literal in the type.

Hero: `clamp(2.5rem, 6.4vw, 4.6rem)`, letter-spacing `-0.025em`, line-height 1.03. `text-wrap: balance` on headings, body capped near 68ch.

## Layout

- Hero: two columns at `1180px` max, collapsing to one under 900px. The landing sections share the hero's `1180px` container so left edges align down the page.
- Application pages use an 800px reading column; operations uses a 232px queue rail plus a fluid work area, collapsing to a horizontal scroller under 880px.
- Radii stay small: 4px on controls, 6 to 8px on the hero objects. Nothing is over-rounded.
- Wide content (tables, code) scrolls inside its own container so the page body never scrolls sideways.

## Imagery

Two Unsplash photographs, both verified to resolve:

- `photo-1622618991746-fe6004db3a47`, warm gold perfume still life, the hero's main object.
- `photo-1772987714654-2df39af2c658`, skincare bottles on a shop shelf, overlapped top-left. It is the literal referent of "UK shelves".

The hero's third object is a **price tag** attached to the photograph, showing product, UK delivery, fee and flight, ending on the Harare total. It is generated by `quoteCalc()`, the same function that produces a real quote, so it cannot drift from the business rules. Showing the whole arithmetic in the first fold is the point: the product's promise is a price you know before you pay.

## Motion

One orchestrated page-load, nothing on scroll. Headline, sub, form and trust list rise and fade on a 50ms stagger with `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; the art fades in behind them. Everything is wrapped in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, so reduced-motion users get the finished layout with no animation at all rather than a suppressed transition. Content is never gated behind an animation.

## What this deliberately is not

- **Not the editorial-typographic lane.** No mono eyebrows above every section, no hairline rule dividers, no monochrome restraint. That lane is saturated.
- **Not a SaaS landing page.** No gradient hero, no three icon cards, nothing centred.
- **Not a money-transfer app.** No flags, no exchange-rate widget, no blue-and-green. Being mistaken for a remittance service is a commercial risk.
- **Not a cargo forwarder.** No clipart aeroplanes, no red and yellow, no rate tables competing for attention.

## Accessibility

Every hero colour pair is contrast-checked in both themes, minimum 3:1 for large text and 4.5:1 for body. Lowest measured pair is the hero seam at 3.33:1 against a 3:1 requirement; body text sits between 5.08:1 and 16.38:1.

The `--gold` accent is fixed across themes precisely because the hero ground never lightens; using the theme-flipping `--ochre` there would have dropped it to unreadable in light mode.
